Teach your car to see the road.

Train road signs on your laptop, send them to the phone on your car, and watch it drive itself. It slows down in school zones and stops at zebra crossings, following rules you set.

Built for real roads (and classrooms)

Everything an autonomous car needs

Vision on the phone, control on the ESP32, and your laptop as mission control.

Teach by showing

Hold a sign up to the camera for a few seconds. Cogno Pilot learns its shape and colours, plus any words printed on it.

Rules you decide

School zone? Cap speed at 40%. Zebra crossing? Stop for 3 seconds, then go. Every object gets its own rule.

The phone is the brain

Google MediaPipe vision runs on the phone itself. The car gets drive commands over Bluetooth and its status shows on the LCD.

Mission control

Pair over Wi-Fi with one tap. Stream the car's view live, switch modes and take over whenever you want.

How it works

From zero to self-driving in three steps

Open on both devices

Choose Vehicle on the phone and Control Station on the laptop, using the same fleet code. The phone shows up on the laptop right away.

Train objects & set rules

Record samples of each sign with the laptop camera, add the text printed on it, and choose what the car should do when it sees it.

Send & go live

Send the training to the vehicle and switch to Live. The car drives on its own while you watch its camera with every detection marked.
